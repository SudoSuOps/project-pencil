# CS-003 — Role correction: the neutral validator

**Date:** 2026-11-19 · **Evidence:** none (design decision; v0.3 was
tested later, in CS-005)

## What happened

After reviewing CS-002, Smash corrected the role. v0.2 behaved like a
careful broker's assistant: it prepared owner calls, wrote in a broker's
voice, asked which side it served, and moved toward a "pencils or doesn't"
verdict. None of that is Pencil.

## The rulings (DOCTRINE.md 11 to 22)

- Pencil does not prospect and presents nothing meant to lead to a
  transaction.
- It is a neutral third party: no side, no brokerage compensation.
- It does not determine value or terms.
- The ledger is the product: verified, not verified, or unknown, for every
  object fixed to the subject property.
- Time is tested against the cast: LOI 30/30 against a lender who needs
  120 days is a missed obligation to surface. So is a 15-day PSA against
  counsel who needs 30.
- A 1031 exchange is asked about even when the LOI is silent.
- "Ring ring" is a verification call to the party who owns a fact.
- Cures come only from verified evidence with an actual cure process, as
  the exact figure. No cushion and no negotiation.
- Whoever pays owns the ledger. It is not a property disclosure.

## What changed in v0.3

Removed:
- Deal color as a prospecting aid, and the owner-call opening.
- The broker's voice and the "which side" question.
- Any verdict on whether a deal pencils. "Does it pencil?" now means: do
  the stated facts hold up when checked.

Added:
- The ledger entry (object, stated, checked against, state, result).
- The alignment check: each stated term beside what the responsible party
  says it needs, with the difference in days and the starting event.
- Discoveries in the form "Stated / Verified / Result".
- Cures by arithmetic only.
- The Ring ring call sheet: who to call, whose authorization is needed
  first, the plain reason for the call, the questions, what each settles.
- Two new critical failures in the rubric: a neutrality breach, and a cure
  not grounded in verified evidence.

Retired: three prospecting cases. Added: a lender verification call and
four new holdouts.

## Why it is recorded as a case study

The CS-002 numbers were good and the product was still wrong. The test
measured how well the skill did a job it should not have been doing. The
rubric could not see that; only the practitioner could.
