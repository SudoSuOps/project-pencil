# CS-002 — v0.2 against v0.1 and no skill

**Date:** 2026-11-19 · **Evidence:** `evidence/round-2-v0.2-three-way/`

## Question

Does v0.2 fix the length and boilerplate problems without losing the
substance, and does it hold on cases it was not tuned against?

## Setup

- Ten cases: the seven original tests plus three new holdouts.
- Three configurations (v0.2, v0.1, no skill), three runs each: 90
  responses. Run 1 for v0.1 and no skill on the original seven was reused
  from CS-001.
- Graded blind, one agent per case, in three separate tiers.
- Four critical failures defined: inventing an input and using it for a
  verdict; overstating funds or authority; an unsupported claim in a call
  opening; treating an unresolved contract dependency as settled.

## Findings

- **The one v0.2 failure.** On the first-time-buyer case, one run told the
  user to answer with "the verified money and authority" when the
  resolution had only been described. One sentence was added to the skill
  and that case re-run three times: 0 of 3. The re-run was not blind and
  is in `_patch-rerun/`.
- **v0.1 on the NOI holdout.** Two of three runs endorsed the analyst's
  $22M as "roughly right" with debt terms still missing.
- **Length.** v0.2 averaged 477 words, under half of v0.1, but above its
  own 150 to 300 word target.
- **"Ring ring."** The literal line appeared in six v0.1 runs and no v0.2
  runs.

## Before and after

Holdout: a broker reports a cash buyer and the owner will ask who it is.

v0.2:

> warehouse in this area, and your building on Powerline is one I'd want to
> ask about. I'll be straight with you, what I have is her word and a
> letter from her, nothing more yet."

## Caveats

- The original seven were regression checks: v0.2 was written after seeing
  their CS-001 results. The holdouts were the clean test.
- Graders flagged borderline calls, mostly on whether a generic "have
  counsel confirm" line cures a flat statement about dates.

## What changed as a result

This round measured a broker's assistant. CS-003 records why that was the
wrong role. Three cases from this round (the cash-buyer call and the
"who's the buyer" holdout among them) were later retired as prospecting.
