# Pain points

The problems the Pencil exists to surface, as Smash has described them
from practice. Each is recorded as stated. "Source" says what supports it
today; most are practitioner statements with no data on file yet.

Smash has said this list is longer than what is here. Add to it as he
names them.

| # | Pain point | As described | What the Pencil does about it | Source | Doctrine |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The unwind period | A seller starts at a price that cannot be defended. The property unwinds over days on market, successive exclusive listing firms, and price adjustments, and closes far below the ask. Example: $10M ask, $6M close, 300 days, three firms. | Verifies the ingredients before the listing so the starting number can be defended, and shows which cannot be yet. Does not set the price. | Practitioner statement; one example | 39; CS-008 |
| 2 | Sellers not in defendable mode | Sellers go fishing for a round or two before the pain sinks in. Brokers who take those assignments absorb the cost. | Builds the seller's About You from checkable facts so a broker can decide whether to take the assignment. | Practitioner statement | 40; CS-008 |
| 3 | Terms the cast cannot deliver | LOI gives 30-day diligence and 30-day close with a hard deposit; the lender needs 120 days. LOI says PSA in 15 days; counsel needs 30. | Sets each stated term beside what the responsible party says it needs, and reports the difference. | Practitioner statement | 17 |
| 4 | The exchange nobody mentioned | A buyer is in a 1031 and must close property A to close property B, and the LOI says nothing. | Asks even when the documents are silent; logs an omitted exchange as a discovery. | Practitioner statement | 18 |
| 5 | Claims taken at their word | "Cash buyer", "financing is lined up", a broker's letter about a client's funds. | A statement verifies only that it was said. Capital is placed at the stage the evidence supports. | Practitioner statement; CS-002 | 2, 3 |
| 6 | Confidential documents leaving the NDA | A seller sends documents to brokers for an opinion of value. The brokers are not hired as agents; they sign an NDA. A broker then sends the documents to a cloud service for underwriting, which Smash describes as a violation of the NDA. | The vault: confidential documents are held there and the Pencil works on them without sending them out. Custody is recorded. | Practitioner statement. Whether a given act breaches a given NDA depends on its wording and is counsel's question. | 29, 35, 41 |
| 7 | Talking about a deal under NDA | A broker who received documents under an NDA tells a client he has a deal coming in. | Not yet addressed. Access scoped to the assignment and a custody record are the nearest pieces. | Practitioner statement | 35 |

## What would make each one defendable

A pain point stated from experience is a claim like any other. For each,
the record should eventually hold: how often it happens, what it costs,
and a real example with identifying details removed.
