## What changed

## Where that leaves you

Re-trade to roughly **$17.2M**, or get comfortable that a 6.22% cap is market for this asset and that there is enough rent growth to carry a 3.7% starting cash yield.

Two things to check before you rely on the $1.15M:

1. **What the recovery was for.** If the related loss or repair cost was also expensed in the T-12, removing only the recovery understates NOI, and that expense should come out too.
2. **Whether there are other one-time items.** One found usually means the T-12 has not been scrubbed. Ask for the general ledger and check insurance and tax at your basis, not the seller's.
