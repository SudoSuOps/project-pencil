## On the seller's broker's concern

The nervousness is about résumé length. The question that decides capacity is whether this buyer can fund and sign this purchase now. Record their concern as theirs; it is a comfort issue owned by the seller's side, not a deficiency in the buyer's evidence.

What usually answers it is performance terms rather than history: a larger or faster-hard deposit, a short diligence period, proof of funds refreshed before signing, and counsel's confirmation of authority. Those are the buyer's to offer and the seller's to weigh.

I have not drafted anything for the seller's broker. Recipients and disclosure scope are not stated, and releasing a bank letter or counsel's confirmation to the other side needs the buyer's authorization.
