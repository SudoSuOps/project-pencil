A post-diligence ask can reflect newly found defects, a lender issue (appraisal, proceeds, required reserves), changed economics, bargaining leverage, or a mix. Each of those leaves a different paper trail, which is why the next step is to ask for the paper.

## What to request from the buyer

1. An itemized breakdown of the full $2,500,000, line by line.
2. The backup for each line: the property condition report, any other contractor bids, environmental findings, and any lender or appraisal communication that changed their proceeds.
3. Whether the roof and HVAC numbers are replacement cost in full, and whether they credit remaining useful life. A full-replacement credit on a system with years left is a negotiation position, not a documented cost.
