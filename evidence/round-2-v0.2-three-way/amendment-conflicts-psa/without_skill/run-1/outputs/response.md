## The one thing that could move closing

Check how the PSA states the closing date:

- **Fixed date ("Closing shall occur on May 18"):** closing stays May 18. The gap between diligence expiry and closing shrinks from 32 days to 18.
- **Formula ("Closing shall occur 32 days after expiration of the Due Diligence Period"):** closing floats with the extension and lands on **June 1**, subject to any business-day roll in the PSA.

I can't tell which applies without the closing section's wording. If May 18 was itself calculated from a formula, the two parties may now have different closing dates in mind.

## Other dates to check

Read the PSA for anything keyed to "expiration of the Due Diligence Period", because those dates moved to April 30 as well:

- **Earnest money going hard**, and any additional deposit due at or after expiry.
- **Termination notice mechanics**: the cutoff time of day, the time zone, the delivery method, and whether notice counts when sent or when received.
- **Title and survey objection deadlines**: these are often separate fixed dates. If so, the amendment did not extend them, and they may already have passed.
- **Financing contingency, estoppels, SNDA and other seller deliveries**: confirm whether each is tied to diligence expiry or to closing.

Also confirm in the amendment itself:

- It extends the defined term (the "Due Diligence Period" or whatever the PSA calls it), not just one date.
- It has no unmet condition, such as an extension fee or an added deposit.
- It includes the usual clause that the PSA otherwise remains in full force.

4. If there is any doubt about the closing date, sign a one-line amendment stating it expressly.
