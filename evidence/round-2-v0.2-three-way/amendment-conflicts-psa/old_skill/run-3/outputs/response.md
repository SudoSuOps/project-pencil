## What to check in the documents

1. **How the PSA defines closing.** If it is a fixed date of May 18, it stays put. If it is defined as a number of days after diligence expires, the extension may have moved it and the amendment didn't need to say so. Read the definition, not the summary.
2. **Everything else keyed to diligence expiry.** These may or may not have moved with it, depending on whether the PSA ties them to the defined diligence period or to a hard date:
   - when the deposit goes hard, and any additional deposit
   - title and survey objection deadlines
   - any financing contingency
   - termination notice deadlines
   - extension options
3. **The amendment's own terms.** Look for a clause confirming the rest of the PSA is unchanged, and for any condition attached to the extension (a fee, an added deposit, a release of earlier deposit).
4. **Day-count rules.** Calendar or business days, and what happens when a deadline lands on a weekend or holiday. Not stated in what you gave me, so it stays open.
