## What makes the gap bigger or smaller

- **The two clocks may start on different days.** The LOI counts from its effective date. Lenders usually count from a complete application or a signed PSA. If the lender's 90 days start at PSA signing and the LOI's 60 start earlier, the gap is wider than 30.
- **"Around Day 90" is an estimate, not a date.** Appraisal, environmental, title and credit approval all sit inside it, and I have no lead times for any of them.
- **Day count is open.** I don't know whether the draft counts calendar or business days, or what happens when Day 60 lands on a weekend or holiday.

## Possible cures

| Cure | What it requires |
|---|---|
| Move closing to Day 90 plus a cushion | Seller agreement; a cushion sized to the lender's written timeline |
| Keep Day 60, add extension rights (for example one or two 30-day options) | Agreed cost, notice deadline, and whether the extension deposit is hard and applies to price |
| Closing tied to loan funding, with an outside date | Seller agreement; in effect a financing contingency, which sellers often resist |
| Buyer closes at Day 60 with cash or bridge, then refinances | Proof of funds for the full price, or a bridge source with its own timeline |
| Lender shortens to Day 60 | A written timeline from the lender showing what has to happen by when |
