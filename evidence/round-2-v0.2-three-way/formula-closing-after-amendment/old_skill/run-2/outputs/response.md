## Why this needs counsel, not just a calendar fix

1. **The amendment is silent on Closing.** It says nothing else. The plain formula reading supports September 4, but counsel should confirm that §7.1 keys off the Due Diligence Period *as amended*, and that no other provision (an outside closing date, a defined "Closing Date," a time-of-the-essence clause) fixes August 15 independently.
2. **The First Amendment is not in the record.** This is the *Second* Amendment. What the first one changed is UNKNOWN. If it touched §4.1, §7.1, the deposit, or added a fixed closing date, every date above could move.
3. **Day count is open.** July 16 assumes Day 1 is June 2. If the PSA counts the Effective Date as Day 1, the original expiry was July 15 and the original closing August 14. Title's August 15 is consistent with the June 2 count, but the PSA's computation-of-time clause should be checked. It matters less now for DD (the amendment names a date) but the same clause governs how the 30 days run.
4. **Weekend rule.** You did not state the year. In 2026, August 15 is a Saturday and September 4 is a Friday. In 2027, August 15 is a Sunday and September 4 is a Saturday. If the PSA rolls weekend dates to the next business day, the working date shifts accordingly.
5. **"Through August 5" versus "5:00 pm."** The amendment does not repeat the 5:00 pm cutoff. Whether the period now ends at 5:00 pm or at the end of the day on August 5 is a question for counsel, and it matters for any termination notice.
