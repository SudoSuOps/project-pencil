## What is open

1. **Which closing date controls.** The amendment extends diligence and says nothing else. Read literally, §7.1 follows the extended period to September 4. A party could argue the intent was to move diligence only and leave closing at August 15. I can show the two readings; which one is operative is counsel's call. This is the item that matters, and it should be answered in writing. If the parties mean September 4, a one-line confirmation from counsel to title fixes the calendar. If they mean August 15, there is a 3-day funding gap to cure now.
2. **The First Amendment.** This is the Second Amendment, so there is a first one I have not seen. If it touched §4.1, §7.1, the deposit, or any extension right, every date above could change.
3. **Day counting.** I counted calendar days with June 2 as Day 1. I do not have the PSA's rule on calendar versus business days or on deadlines that land on a weekend or holiday. You did not give the year, so I did not test weekdays. Worth a look: in 2026, August 15 is a Saturday.
4. **"Through August 5."** The original expiry had a 5:00 pm cutoff. The amendment gives a date with no time. Whether the 5:00 pm cutoff carries over is a counsel question, and it matters if any notice goes out on the last day.
5. **Deposit and notices.** If the deposit goes hard or a termination notice is due at diligence expiry, those moved to August 5 on the same logic. I have not seen those sections.
6. **Lender's date.** "Can fund August 18" is a statement, not a commitment. Where the loan sits (term sheet, commitment, conditions cleared) is not in what you gave me, and neither is whether the lender is working toward August 15 or a later closing.

## Who needs to hear what

- **Counsel:** confirm the operative closing date under §7.1 as amended, and the day-count and time-of-day points.
- **Title company:** is holding August 15. Once counsel confirms, title needs the confirmed date from the parties. I would not have them move the calendar on my arithmetic.
- **Lender:** needs the confirmed closing date, plus any rate lock or commitment expiry checked against it. A September 4 closing only helps if the lender's paper stays good that long.
