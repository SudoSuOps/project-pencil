## The math

| Step | Original | After Second Amendment |
|---|---|---|
| Effective Date | June 1 | June 1 |
| Due Diligence Period expires (4.1) | July 16, 5:00 pm (45th day after June 1) | August 5 |
| Closing (7.1: 30 days after expiration) | **August 15** | **September 4** |

Section 7.1 sets closing by formula, not by a fixed date. The amendment moved the expiration to August 5 and said nothing about closing, so the formula carries closing with it: August 5 plus 30 days is September 4.

The amendment was signed July 10, before the original July 16 expiration, so it extended a live period rather than reviving a dead one.

## What this means

- **Lender:** funding on August 18 is 17 days ahead of the contractual closing date. There is no default problem.
- **Title company:** August 15 is the old date and needs to be corrected. If anyone treats August 15 as the outside date, a buyer who is not funded until August 18 looks late when it is not.
- **Closing on August 18:** that would be an early closing. It needs the seller's agreement unless the PSA lets the buyer accelerate on notice.

## Things to check in the documents

1. **First Amendment.** A Second Amendment implies a First, which I have not seen. If it fixed a closing date or changed 4.1 or 7.1, the answer could change.
2. **Seller's reading.** The seller could argue the parties only meant to extend diligence and leave closing at August 15. That is a weak argument against a formula, but the amendment is silent, so get it in writing: a one-line email or Third Amendment saying "Closing Date is September 4" (or whatever date you agree).
3. **Time of day on August 5.** The amendment says "through August 5" with no time. Section 4.1's 5:00 pm cutoff arguably still applies. Treat 5:00 pm on August 5 as the deadline for any termination notice.
4. **Counting and business-day rules.** I counted June 2 as day 1, which gives July 16. Check the PSA's time-computation clause, and whether deadlines falling on a weekend or holiday roll forward. Check what day of the week September 4 is in your year; it sits next to Labor Day weekend.
5. **Other dates keyed to diligence expiration.** The deposit going hard, any additional deposit, title and survey objection deadlines, and any financing contingency likely moved to August 5 as well.
6. **Loan commitment and rate lock.** If you close September 4 rather than August 18, confirm both run that far.
