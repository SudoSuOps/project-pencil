**On the text you quoted, closing is September 4, not August 15.** The title company's date is the pre-amendment date and is stale; the lender's August 18 funding fits comfortably inside the amended timeline.

## The math

| Clock | Original | After Second Amendment |
|---|---|---|
| Effective Date | June 1 | June 1 |
| Due Diligence expires (§4.1) | July 16, 5:00 pm (45th day after June 1) | August 5 |
| Closing (§7.1: 30 days after DD expiration) | August 15 | **September 4** |

- **Original DD expiry:** June 2 is day 1, June 30 is day 29, so day 45 is July 16.
- **Original closing:** July 16 plus 30 days is August 15. That is where the title company's date comes from.
- **Amended closing:** Section 7.1 is a formula, not a fixed date. The amendment moved the Due Diligence expiration to August 5 and left 7.1 alone, so closing moves with it: August 5 plus 30 days is September 4.
- **Amendment timing:** it was signed July 10, before the July 16 expiry, so it extended a live period rather than reviving a lapsed one.

## What to do now

1. **Get the closing date confirmed in writing by both sides.** The amendment never mentions closing, and a silent amendment is how one party ends up believing August 15 still holds. If the other side reads it that way and the lender cannot fund until August 18, you have a default argument. A one-line email confirmation from both counsel, or a short Third Amendment saying "Closing Date is September 4," closes that gap.
2. **Tell the title company to move the closing to September 4** and send them the Second Amendment. Have them refresh the commitment, prorations and settlement statement to the new date.
3. **Decide whether you want to close early.** As quoted, §7.1 sets closing at 30 days after expiration, not "on or before." If you want to close when the lender can fund (August 18 or soon after), you need an early-closing right elsewhere in the PSA or the other side's written agreement.

## Things I can't see that could change the answer

- **The First Amendment.** This is the *Second* Amendment. If the first one touched the DD period or fixed a closing date, the math above changes.
- **A defined "Closing Date" elsewhere.** If the PSA or an earlier amendment states a calendar date for closing instead of relying on the §7.1 formula, the extension did not move it automatically.
- **Time of day on August 5.** "Through August 5" does not repeat the 5:00 pm cutoff. Treat 5:00 pm on August 5 as the deadline for any termination notice unless the parties clarify.
- **Business-day rollover.** You did not give the year. If September 4 lands on a weekend or holiday, check whether the PSA rolls deadlines to the next business day.
- **Other clocks keyed to DD expiration.** Deposit going hard, an additional deposit, title and survey objection deadlines, the financing contingency, estoppel or SNDA delivery, and any extension options likely all moved to August 5 too. Check each one.
- **Lender-side dates.** Confirm the rate lock and loan commitment run through September 4, not just August 18.
