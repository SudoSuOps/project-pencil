# ALIGNMENT CHECK: LOI clock vs. lender letter

**Finding:** Stated closing is Day 75 from the Effective Date. The lender's letter says it anticipates closing within 105 days of receiving a fully executed purchase agreement. Result: the letter does not show the lender can close by Day 75, and its window runs up to 30 days past the stated closing. The two clocks also start from different events, so that 30-day figure is not final.

**Evidence:** LOI text and lender letter dated October 3, both as you quoted them (neither document seen by Pencil). The letter is the lender's own statement of an expectation, not a commitment and not a minimum.

**Next verification:** How many days the lender needs to close, and counted from what event. Only the lender can answer, with the buyer's authorization.

## The math

```
Inspection expiry / deposit non-refundable: Day 45 from Effective Date   [LOI]
Closing: 45 + 30 = Day 75 from Effective Date                            [LOI]
Lender: within 105 days of receipt of a fully executed PSA               [letter, Oct 3]
If the two anchors fall on the same day:
  Lender's outer date minus stated closing:   105 - 75 = 30 days
  Lender's outer date minus deposit going hard: 105 - 45 = 60 days
```

"Within 105 days" is an outer limit. The letter is consistent with a Day 75 closing and equally consistent with a Day 105 closing. It does not say which.
